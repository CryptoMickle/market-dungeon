## Participant protocol

Use the unmodified Production `/judge` route. Do not coach the participant
through the interface and do not explain the two victory conditions before the
run.

1. Assign the next anonymous label from `P01` through `P30`. Keep no mapping to
   a name, handle, wallet, or email in the public evidence.
2. Record only the closed fields in
   [`pilot/PILOT_LOG_TEMPLATE.csv`](pilot/PILOT_LOG_TEMPLATE.csv).
3. Ask the participant to open `/judge`, choose a direction, complete the run,
   and use the independent verifier if they reach a verified result.
4. Do not require a wallet, transaction, social post, or personal information.
5. After the run, ask without prompting:
   - “What two conditions determine whether the run succeeds?”
   - “Did Market Dungeon execute a trade, or did it verify read-only market
     state?”
6. Record only whether each answer demonstrates correct understanding. Do not
   retain free-form answers in the public log.
7. A participant may voluntarily use Continue, share, or challenge. Never ask
   them to publish publicly or connect a wallet.
