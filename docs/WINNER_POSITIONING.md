# Market Dungeon — submission positioning

Updated 11 September 2026 for the v17 Kevin and shared Judge release candidate. The existing Live Judge competition story stays primary; Kevin is a separately selected addition with explicit wallet and evidence boundaries.

**Defeat the boss. Predict the market. Survive both.**

Market Dungeon gives Event Contracts a consequence inside a fantasy roguelite: combat earns the victory, and the verified market outcome decides whether it lasts. Full Expedition and Judge Demo provide a wallet-free way to discover dreamDEX. Somnia Agent Kevin adds an optional paid testnet agent rivalry without making that a prerequisite for playing.

## Three Home choices, two Judge variants

- **Live 1-minute Judge** is the primary competition demo. Choose BTC UP/DOWN against a visible live Shannon testnet target, fight one guard and one boss, then verify the settled result. The timer is the market window, not a combat deadline. A wrong prediction ends this short demo.
- **Historical Replay** is the testnet fallback. Choose before the server draws and seals a finalized market; fight the two encounters and reveal its independently checked settlement. It is labeled historical and does not pretend to predict a live price.
- **Full Expedition** contains 40 rooms, four tiers, 16 monster personas, Kevin, shops, camps and 15 relics. It locks an active five-minute mainnet market before each tier. A wrong omen resurrects the same boss at full strength; a fresh market is required for its rematch. Its combat is locally random, not covered by Judge transcript proof.
- **Somnia Agent Kevin** adds a rival to a separate expedition save. The default simulator is labeled **NO AI**; the player may explicitly connect MetaMask and approve a quoted Shannon testnet STT request to the official LLM agent. Kevin's eligible prediction stays in the header, and the market independently decides both calls. His score never changes combat or rewards.

The neutral Home explains each selected mode before entry. Live and Historical sit inside Judge Demo and share their four-step presentation, recovery controls and result layout. Their live-versus-sealed market semantics and distinct proof formats remain visible.

## Claim boundaries

Full Expedition and both Judge demos need no wallet or transaction; Kevin's real-agent option does. Connection precedes omen lock, and quoted testnet payment requires a separate wallet approval afterward. The server has no wallet or private key. No mode creates a dreamDEX order, redemption or custody flow, and no new custom smart contract is introduced. Continue on dreamDEX opens a separate service. Mainnet market settlement and Shannon agent execution must remain explicit.

Signed receipts authenticate the application environment; they are not independent timestamps or proof that the server was honest. Transcript validation proves rule-valid deterministic Judge actions, not human input. Direct RPC verification establishes the recorded contract state, not a third-party audit. Preview proofs must be verified on the same Preview environment that issued them.
