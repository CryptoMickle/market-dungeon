## Validation record

Final candidate checks and hosted read-only proof round-trips are recorded in the release notes after they run. They cover lint, TypeScript, unit/integration checks, the Shannon proof kernel, optimized build, desktop/mobile browser scenarios, and actual market/RPC verification. Counts must be taken from those completed runs, not copied from a historical checkpoint.

Automated browser checks use real application code with clearly distinguished controlled service fixtures or actual hosted providers. Real settlement checks include exported proof verification and record any transport failures or visible retry actions. They are project-controlled QA, not independent human validation, and do not establish an uptime guarantee.

Three self-reported feedback responses covering Full Expedition and Judge informed the product changes. Device, URL, timing and attempt data do not establish a clean Live Judge conversion denominator. Participant identities and correspondence remain outside the public source. No current trading-volume, revenue or referral-conversion claim is made.
